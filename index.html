OUR PURPOSE

About FreshCheck

A little more independence, every day.

FreshCheck is designed with older and disabled people in mind. It aims to make it easier to keep track of food dates, organise groceries and find simple ways to use ingredients before their dates.

With clear layouts, larger text options and read-aloud support, FreshCheck is designed to make everyday food organisation feel more manageable and help people stay in control of their own kitchen.

Why FreshCheck?

Food labels can be difficult to read and it can be easy to forget what is in the fridge. FreshCheck brings food details and date reminders together in one accessible place.

FreshCheck is a concept prototype. It does not determine whether food is safe to eat.

WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & support

How to use FreshCheck

  1. Open My food to see saved items.
  2. Choose Add food to enter a name, category and date.
  3. Visit Expiration tracker to see upcoming dates.
  4. Open Recipes for simple meal inspiration.
  5. Use Settings to turn on larger text or read-aloud.

Scanning help

Make sure the barcode is visible and the label is well lit. This prototype lets you enter a barcode number or upload a label photo, but it does not decode or identify products automatically.
